# Vocabulary index CEFR references

Some POS-aware CEFR levels in `vocabulary_en.json` and
`vocabulary_mwe_en.json` use `levelSource: "cefr_reference"`. Those assignments
were matched by normalized lemma and part of speech against the following
references. Ambiguous entries with different sense levels were not applied
automatically.

- *The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6*. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University
  of Foreign Studies. Retrieved from
  <https://www.cefr-j.org/data/CEFRJ_wordlist_ver1.6.zip> on 2026-08-24.
- *Word Family Framework of General English*. Developed for the British Council
  by Richard West. Retrieved from
  <https://494f9c0dda672f79b2ee-6b9e395a7343d6a0b8b7ac609388ce35.ssl.cf1.rackcdn.com/wff/>
  on 2026-08-24.

The checked reports and source-file hashes are maintained in the importer under
`docs/reports/cefr-reassessment-en*.md`. The consolidated coverage and remaining
review queue are recorded in `docs/reports/cefr-reassessment-en-summary.md`.
